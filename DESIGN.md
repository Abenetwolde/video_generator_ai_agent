# DESIGN.md — Creative Intent, Audience, and User Testing

## 1. Audience & Creative Intent (Stated Before Generation)
- **Proverb / Story:** *"ድር ቢያብር አንበሳ ያስር"* (*Dir Biyabir Anbesa Yasir* — "When spiderwebs unite, they can tie up a lion"). Told through **Ato Bekele**, an elder highland weaver in Ethiopia, and **Selam**, his young apprentice, over the morning loom and coffee (*jebena buna*).
- **Audience:** Educated Ethiopian and diaspora viewers watching on both **mobile vertical (`9:16`)** and **desktop/YouTube (`16:9`)**.
- **Intent (one sentence):** To remind educated viewers that patient, ordinary daily threads—craft, hospitality, and community solidarity—hold the strength to overcome overwhelming challenges.
- **Target Feeling at the End:** Quiet pride, grounded warmth, and communal resolve.

## 2. Per-Shot Reasons
| Shot | Duration | Size / Camera | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| `shot_01` | 7.5 s | `extreme_wide` / `slow_drift` | Establish the misty Ethiopian highlands at dawn to ground the story in place, stillness, and heritage. |
| `shot_02` | 8.0 s | `medium` / `push_in` | Introduce Ato Bekele at his wooden loom, connecting manual craft to lived wisdom before stating the proverb. |
| `shot_03` | 8.0 s | `extreme_close` / `locked` | Visually prove the fragility of a single thread so the proverb's metaphor feels physical and earned. |
| `shot_04` | 8.0 s | `medium_close` / `pan_right` | Show Selam listening closely and holding the spindle, bridging generational knowledge transfer. |
| `shot_05` | 8.5 s | `close` / `push_in` | Introduce the morning jebena coffee ceremony as an everyday cultural emblem of community gathering. |
| `shot_06` | 8.5 s | `wide` / `slow_drift` | Shift from the quiet loom to shared collective labor in the village courtyard to embody unity in action. |
| `shot_07` | 8.5 s | `insert` / `locked` | Return to the loom's interlocking warp and weft at peak tension to deliver the central proverb directly. |
| `shot_08` | 8.5 s | `medium` / `pull_back` | Reveal the finished white shemma cloth catching warm sunbeams, showing the tangible beauty of patient unity. |
| `shot_09` | 8.5 s | `wide` / `tilt_up` | Leave educated viewers with a timeless emotional horizon linking ancestral craft to modern collective purpose. |

## 3. Name & Place Pronunciation Check (Native Amharic Speaker)
Flagged terms for native-speaker verification during Radio Cut and Near-Final Cut reviews:
- **ድር ቢያብር አንበሳ ያስር** (*Dir Biyabir Anbesa Yasir*) — proverb cadence and pause after *ቢያብር*.
- **ሸማኔ / ሽማ** (*Shemane / Shemma*) — weaver & handwoven cotton cloth.
- **ጀበና / ቡና** (*Jebena / Buna*) — traditional clay coffee pot.
- **ዶርዜ** (*Dorze*) / **ደጋ** (*Dega* highlands) — highland weaving heritage context.
- **አቶ በቀለ** (*Ato Bekele*) & **ሰላም** (*Selam*) — natural Amharic stress and warmth.
