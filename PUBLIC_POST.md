One technical lesson from building the pipeline at \$0 cost: **never pass a character reference sheet blindly to every shot.** When I fed my two-character reference to landscape and macro shots, the image model forced both characters into mountain vistas and coffee pots! Scoping identity references strictly to character shots while sharing a common lighting/film style block restored cinematic shot variety without losing character consistency.
